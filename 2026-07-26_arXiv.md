## Extended Summaries: 2D Materials & Microwave Engineering

### [2607.20032] Huge hole injection in tungsten dichalcogenide heterostructures without electric gating: a DFT study
*Dawid Ciszewski, Wojciech Grochala — cond-mat.mtrl-sci*

This study extends the chemical capacitor concept to van der Waals heterostructures by systematically screening electron acceptors for WS₂- and WSe₂-based TMD–hBN–oxidizer stacks using density functional theory. Through a broad screening of transition-metal dichalcogenides and candidate oxidizers, XeF₂ and KrF₂ were identified as exhibiting type-III broken-gap band alignment with WS₂ and WSe₂, respectively, enabling spontaneous charge transfer without any applied gate voltage. Periodic DFT calculations on large supercells quantify hole concentrations of up to 0.23 h⁺ per W atom in WS₂–hBN–XeF₂ and 0.35 h⁺ per W atom in WSe₂–hBN–KrF₂, among the highest predicted for gate-free doping of 2D materials. These hole concentrations rival electrostatic gating achievable with solid-state ionic gates, opening a route to stable, ultra-high doping of atomically thin semiconductors relevant for 2D electronics and correlated-electron devices.

### [2607.19205] Quantized heat flow in moiré chern bands of bilayer graphene
*Santanu Samai, Debangan Sarkar, Abhijit Halder, T. Taniguchi, K. Watanabe, Subroto Mukerjee, Saurabh Kumar Srivastav, Anindya Das — cond-mat.mes-hall*

This work addresses whether the thermal transport in moiré topological states is as universal as their electrical transport, a question that had remained unexplored despite detailed electrical characterization of Hofstadter spectrum states. Using Johnson-noise thermometry in bilayer graphene aligned to hexagonal boron nitride (moiré wavelength ~14 nm), the authors directly measure the thermal conductance of quantum Hall states, Chern insulator states, and interaction-driven symmetry-broken Chern insulators. They demonstrate that thermal conductance is quantized in units of the thermal conductance quantum κ₀ = π²k_B²T/3h and determined solely by the Chern number, regardless of whether the topological state is driven by the external magnetic field, the moiré potential, or Coulomb interactions. This result establishes the universality of topological heat transport in 2D graphene moiré systems and provides a robust experimental signature for the Chern number applicable to arbitrary many-body topological phases.

### [2607.20335] Stacking-tuned superconductivity and competing charge-density-wave states in NbSe₂
*Sandra Sajan, Xinze Yang, Haojie Guo, … Eduardo H. da Silva Neto, Ravi P. Singh, Maria N. Gastiasoro, Miguel M. Ugeda — cond-mat.supr-con*

This paper demonstrates that the stacking sequence alone—without changing the in-plane atomic structure—can qualitatively transform the collective quantum orders in NbSe₂, a prototypical van der Waals superconductor. Comparing the 4Ha and 2H polytypes, the authors find that 4Ha-NbSe₂ hosts two competing CDW states—a unidirectional commensurate 1Qᶜ phase and a triangular incommensurate 3Qⁱ phase—absent in the 2H polytype, revealing how interlayer symmetry dictates CDW phenomenology. The interplay between these CDW orders and superconductivity is shown to be strongly polytype-dependent, offering a new design axis for engineering quantum phases in 2D materials beyond layer number and twist angle. These findings underscore NbSe₂ polytypes as a rich platform for studying the competition between charge order and unconventional superconductivity in the 2D limit.

### [2607.20904] Visualizing Microwave-Driven Dynamics of Antiskyrmions and Surface Skyrmions
*Zhuolin Li, Daisuke Nakamura, Spencer Reisbick, Chuhang Liu, Myung-Geun Han, Kosuke Karube, Wataru Koshibae, Naoto Nagaosa, Yasujiro Taguchi, Yimei Zhu, Yoshinori Tokura, Xiuzhen Yu — cond-mat.mtrl-sci*

This study pioneers time-resolved Lorentz transmission electron microscopy to directly observe real-space, picosecond dynamics of a hybrid antiskyrmion structure composed of a central antiskyrmion surrounded by surface skyrmions under microwave excitation. The measurements resolve the temporal evolution of antiskyrmion area and detect second-harmonic signals, providing the first direct evidence of nonlinear collective responses of magnetic topological spin textures to microwave fields. These nonlinear dynamics—including area oscillations and harmonic generation—arise from the interplay between the antiskyrmion's topology and the topology of its surface-skyrmion environment, a mechanism absent in simpler single-skyrmion systems. The results establish microwave-driven topological spin textures as a promising platform for nonlinear magnonics and microwave signal processing at the nanoscale.

### [2607.20102] Frequency-Division Multiplexing in Magnonic Waveguides
*Alina-Cristina Bunea, Laurentiu Stoleriu, Giacomo Talmelli, Dan Neculoiu, Christoph Adelmann, Florin Ciubotaru — cond-mat.mes-hall*

This work experimentally demonstrates frequency-division multiplexing (FDM) of spin waves in a CoFeB magnonic waveguide, a key step toward wave-based information processing using GHz-frequency microwave signals. Two independently generated microwave signals are simultaneously injected into the same waveguide through inductive antennas and characterized using broadband vector network analyzer measurements across a range of bias fields and frequencies. The transmission spectra under single-channel and multiplexed operation show excellent agreement, confirming that spin waves with different frequencies and wavelengths propagate independently without measurable nonlinear interaction in the linear regime. These results validate magnonic FDM as a viable route to multi-channel, on-chip microwave signal processing in future magnonic logic and neuromorphic computing architectures.

### [2607.21147] Magnetically memorable inductance in superconducting multilayer resonators
*R. Tyumenev, D. S. Kalashnikov, B. V. Fradkin, A. A. Neilo, A. G. Shishkin, N. V. Klenov, I. I. Soloviev, A. A. Golubov, M. Yu. Kupriyanov, I. A. Golovchanskiy, V. S. Stolyarov, A. S. Sidorenko, S. V. Bakurskiy — cond-mat.supr-con*

This paper reports fabrication and microwave characterization of split-ring resonators based on Nb/Co/Nb/Co/Nb/Al spin-trigger multilayers, exploiting the spin-valve effect to achieve non-volatile, magnetically programmable kinetic inductance. Reversal of the relative magnetization orientation of the two cobalt layers produces a reproducible resonant frequency shift of up to 4 MHz at zero applied field, corresponding to a measurable change in kinetic inductance of the Nb layers. The non-volatile nature of the effect—persisting without any applied field—makes these resonators attractive for neuromorphic computing circuits that require in situ programmable elements and for quantum circuits requiring tunable microwave resonators without cryogenic control lines. This demonstration of superconductor–ferromagnet hybrid microwave resonators with magnetic memory advances the integration of classical spin-valve physics with quantum circuit architecture.

### [2607.20096] Power-Law Suppression of Superfluid Stiffness in High-Kinetic-Inductance NbN Films
*Meenakshi Sharma, Hrishikesh Borah, Sandeep Singh, Berit H. Goodge, Edouard Lesne, Sandra Nestler, Surinder P. Singh, Haolin Jin, Yejin Lee, Bernd Büchner, Uri Vool — cond-mat.supr-con / cond-mat.mes-hall*

This study uses ultrathin NbN films (2.8–25 nm) patterned into microwave resonators with sheet kinetic inductance up to 300 pH/□ to probe how extreme disorder reshapes the superconducting state. In the thinnest films, electrical transport reveals signatures of a Berezinskii-Kosterlitz-Thouless transition, indicating two-dimensional superconducting behavior. Strikingly, the microwave response reveals a pronounced power-law suppression of the superfluid stiffness at low temperatures, fundamentally inconsistent with the Mattis-Bardeen theory of conventional dirty-limit superconductors and suggesting exotic pairing correlations in the strongly disordered limit. These results are directly relevant to the design of high-impedance superconducting quantum circuits, where NbN kinetic inductance elements are widely used, and indicate that standard modeling assumptions break down in the ultra-high-inductance regime.
